## Execution plan

### C — the bake-off: job T1 and its re-run T1b
**T1 (job 7143997, dll-4gpu1, 18 October; translator `0a64328`):**

| Stage        | Outcome                                                                                                                                                           |
|--------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 10 `lindat`  | **The baseline.** 1,230 segments (37 metadata fields + 1,193 ALTO blocks), 0 failed, terms 229/232, numbers 0.993, length ratio 1.03, 2,508 s                     |
| 20/21 `ct2`  | Every segment failed with `libcublas.so.12 is not found`, yet both stages were marked ok: CTranslate2 4.8.3 needs CUDA 12 cuBLAS, and the venv's torch is CUDA 13 |
| 30 LLM       | vLLM 0.31 loaded Qwen3.8-27B (bf16, 50 GiB), then refused `max_num_seqs` 1024 (Qwen3.8 is hybrid; 639 Mamba cache blocks)                                         |
| 50 samples   | The same cuBLAS error                                                                                                                                             |
| 60 live lane | 4 passed, 3 stub-only skips: #46's ÚFAL step                                                                                                                      |

**Follow-up (10-18 evening):**
- **`ct2`:** `warm()` runs one short generation after the load, so a model that loads but cannot run stops the service and the CLI at start-up. The message names the fix (`nvidia-cublas-cu12` on `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, a CUDA 12 runtime, or `CT2_DEVICE=cpu`).
- **`eval/bakeoff.py`** exits 2 when a variant cannot start, fails `--max-consecutive-failures` (10) segments in a row with the same error (nothing written), or fails every segment.
- **`load_vocab.py`** writes LF; the cluster's sha `1d7af821…` is the LF file.
- **The job:**
  - `flock` per `OUT`;
  - cuBLAS 12 from the venv, else `CUDA_MODULE`, and the `ct2` stages are skipped without it;
  - `--max-num-seqs 64`;
  - each run clears its label's old outputs; only finished runs (`runs/<label>.done`) are combined;
  - Qwen's model-card licence goes into `code_state`.

**T1b:**
1. Push the follow-up. On the cluster: `git restore data_samples/in-place_translated_files/alto`, then fetch and merge `test`.
2. Prep on a networked node: `STEPS="venv check" bash issue4_bakeoff_prep.sh`. It adds `nvidia-cublas-cu12`.
3. `FORCE=1 ONLY="20 21 30 40 50 90" sbatch -w dll-4gpu1 issue4_bakeoff_job.sh`. LINDAT, the live lane and the tests stay from T1.
4. Archive back: `report`, `runs`, `logs`, `code_state`. The samples go into `data_samples/ct2_translated_files/xml` (and LINDAT's `in-place_translated_files/xml`), and the combined CSVs into `eval/results/issue4_bakeoff_2610/`.

**T1b (job 7144164, 10-18 evening; translator `54d81cf`):**
- **LLM arms** (Qwen3.8-27B bf16): 99 of 1,230 segments failed in both, exactly the 99 with a vocabulary term, all `HTTP 400.`
  - The glossary went as a second system message, which Qwen3's chat template refuses.
  - So terms 0/232, and desc0 = desc300.
  - Without a glossary, the 1,131 others: numbers 0.999, length ratio 1.01, 0.96 char similarity to LINDAT, 30 chars/s (LINDAT 20).
- **`ct2`:** `CUBLAS_STATUS_NOT_SUPPORTED` on every segment. The RTX PRO 6000 is a Blackwell GPU, and the job used `module cuda/12.3` (the venv had no `nvidia-cublas-cu12`). The bake-off stopped each variant after 10 identical errors and wrote nothing (exit 2), but `warm()`'s 4-token probe had passed.
- **Samples:** the same error; the LINDAT samples were never attempted.
- **Qwen3.8-27B and -FP8:** the model card says `license: apache-2.0`.

**The fixes (10-19, delivered in chat):**
- one system message (instructions, then the glossary);
- a provider's refusal quoted in the error (`HTTP 400: …`, `processors/http_retry.provider_message`);
- the `ct2` probe runs a record-sized prompt through the chat template, and `NOT_SUPPORTED` names the Blackwell fix (cuBLAS ≥ 12.8, or an Ampere/Hopper GPU);
- the job's samples stage runs LINDAT first.

**T1c:**
1. Prep `STEPS="venv check"` (`nvidia-cublas-cu12` into `venv-bakeoff`).
2. `FORCE=1 ONLY="30 40 90" sbatch -w dll-4gpu1 …` (the LLM arms, bf16 as in T1b).
3. Then `--dependency=afterany:<that job>`: `FORCE=1 ONLY="20 21 40 50 90" sbatch -p gpu-troja …` (`ct2` and the samples on an Ampere GPU).
